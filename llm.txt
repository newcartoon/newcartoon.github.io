# Benny & Friends Adventure

Official website for the family-friendly kids cartoon and animation series 'Benny & Friends Adventure'.
URL: https://newcartoon.github.io/

## Quick Summary
Benny & Friends Adventure is an animated cartoon series for kids focusing on learning, friendship stories, and magical journeys. The series features Benny the Bear and his team of animal friends following a mysterious treasure map.

- **Benny the Bear**: The cheerful leader wearing denim overalls.
- **Rosie Rabbit**: Bright, gentle friend in a pink dress.
- **Polly Parrot**: Aerial scout wearing a bold green vest.
- **Toby Turtle**: Wise riddle decoder with custom cheek stripes.
